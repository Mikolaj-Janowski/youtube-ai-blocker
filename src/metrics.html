Precision Of all auto-blocked videos, what fraction was a correct block?

Recall Of all videos that should have been blocked, what fraction was caught?

F1 Score Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall — overall balance.
2 × P × R / (P + R)
Higher = better overall filtering
⚠️ Measurement methodology: True Positives are estimated as (auto-blocked − false positives). False Positives are recorded when you click "Show this" on an auto-blocked video. False Negatives are recorded when you manually block a video with ≥ 40% similarity to existing blocked items (content the filter should have caught automatically).
